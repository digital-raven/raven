# Gaming

Gaming requires [enabling unfree software](operators-manual.md).

## Steam

Steam has a native Linux client. When you launch and log-in to Steam, go
to `Steam -> Settings -> Compatibility` and "Enable Steam Play for all other titles".
This will tell Steam to use Proton for all titles in your library. Valve has done
a ton of great work for cross-platform game compatibility.

## Lutris

Lutris handles a collection of other publishers and can also launch generic
Windows .exe files. Use it for games from places like Itch.io
or anywhere else you get regular .exe installers.

## Heroic

Heroic has an entry under unfree because of a rumored SteamDeck-like
experience. Lutris > Heroic, but Heroic handles GoG just fine.

The Heroic launcher itself is FOSS, but it serves primarily as a launcher
for unfree software.

## Good Vendors

Steam have been solid for over 20 years at time of writing.

GoG sells DRM-free titles. GoG also distributes optional offline installers.
Lutris should handle these, but YMMV per game.
